# Figure sources

These PNG crops reproduce figures from the supplied papers. The shortened
`report.tex` uses only the Cai architecture crop, with a caption crediting its
authors and original figure number. The other crops remain reference assets.
Figures were rendered at 300 dpi and cropped to exclude surrounding text and
the original captions. Diagram contents were not redrawn or modified.

| Asset | Paper | Original figure | PDF page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `schifanella2016_architecture.png` | Schifanella et al., *Detecting Sarcasm in Multimodal Social Platforms* (2016) | 3 | 7 |
| `poria2016_architecture.png` | Poria et al., *A Deeper Look into Sarcastic Tweets Using Deep Convolutional Neural Networks* (2016) | 2 | 5 |
| `cai2019_architecture.png` | Cai et al., *Multi-Modal Sarcasm Detection in Twitter with Hierarchical Fusion Model* (2019) | 2 | 4 |
| `tian2023_architecture.png` | Tian et al., *Dynamic Routing Transformer Network for Multimodal Sarcasm Detection* (2023) | 2 | 3 |
| `jia2024_augmentation.png` | Jia et al., *Debiasing Multimodal Sarcasm Detection with Contrastive Learning* (2024) | 2 | 3 |

The unused Jia figure illustrates counterfactual augmentation, not a complete
model architecture.

Copyright remains with the respective rights holders. Citation is attribution,
not a license grant. Reuse and redistribution permissions have not been verified.

`clip_pipeline.tex` is an original TikZ vector schematic of the local CLIP
attribute-extraction intervention. It is compiled directly by `report.tex` and
does not depict measured attention weights or an observed example.
